# Tone discrimination learning in mice

A made-up paper for the miragold example audit. Pages are separated by form feeds; a real audit uses the published PDF's text layer.

## Methods

### Subjects and task

Twelve mice were trained to report which of two tones (4 or 8 kHz) was played, in 10 daily sessions of 30 trials each.

Trials with response times longer than 2 s were excluded from analysis. Accuracy is the percentage of correct trials in each session.

## Results

### Learning

Mice were trained to discriminate two tones (4 and 8 kHz).

Twelve mice completed 3,600 trials over 10 sessions. Accuracy rose from 60% in session 1 to 87% in session 10 (Figure 1).
